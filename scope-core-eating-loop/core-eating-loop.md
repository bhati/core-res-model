# Core Eating Loop

The Core Eating Loop turns isolated food logs into a living understanding of how a person eats.

---

## What It Solves

People eat every day but rarely see the shape of their eating. Meal logging tools focus on what was eaten — calories, macros, nutrients. They miss the pattern: when someone eats, what their typical day looks like, how consistent or fluid their rhythm is.

The Core Eating Loop makes this pattern visible. Not through analytics or dashboards, but through a system that gradually learns a person's eating behavior and mirrors it back to them.

---

## What It Is

A behavioral awareness engine. It watches food logs accumulate and builds three kinds of understanding:

1. **What you eat.** The foods, what you call them, your usual portions, your common pairings.
2. **When you eat.** Your meals, your timing, your occasions — breakfast, lunch, that 4pm chai.
3. **How your day unfolds.** The expected rhythm of your eating day — what's happened, what's coming, what's missing.

This understanding starts rough and gets personal. The more you log, the less you need to explain. The system fills in what it already knows and only asks about what it doesn't.

---

## What It Is Not

- Not a calorie tracker.
- Not a macro tracker.
- Not a diet compliance system.
- Not an analytics dashboard.

Nutrition can sit on top later. The loop itself is purely behavioral.

---

## How It Feels

**Day 1:** You type "had chai and paratha." The system stores it and takes its best guess at what you mean.

**Day 3:** You type "chai." The system already knows your chai, your usual quantity, and that you tend to have it around 8am.

**Day 7:** You type "chai." The system knows everything. No guesses needed. Your day view shows what you've eaten, what's expected next, and a quiet observation: "5th day of chai-paratha mornings."

Logging becomes lighter. Awareness becomes sharper.

---

## The Product

The product is awareness, not metrics.

The user gets:
- A mirror of how they actually eat
- Reduced logging friction over time
- A sense of daily eating rhythm
- Gentle presence without judgment

---

## The Essence

The Core Eating Loop turns isolated food logs into a living understanding of a person's eating rhythm — and keeps refining that understanding every day.
