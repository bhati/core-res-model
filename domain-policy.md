Domain Policy

(Design-Level Placeholder — Pending Vertical Instantiation)

⸻

The Domain Model defines what can exist and happen. The Domain Context captures what has happened and what is true. The User Context defines who the person is and what they need. What remains undefined is how these inputs should shape the domain's behavior for a specific user at a specific time. This is the role of policy — the bridge that reads context and configures the domain model into an appropriate, stable, and safe operating mode.

Policy is not yet specified at the horizontal level. The current directional thinking is that policy will take the form of structured rules — with triggers drawn from context, conditions evaluated against the domain model, and actions that modulate how states, transitions, entities, artifacts, and tools operate. Rather than prescribing this framework abstractly, we intend to derive it from a concrete vertical: a complete nutrition policy set will be authored first, and the horizontal policy framework will be extracted from that instantiation.
